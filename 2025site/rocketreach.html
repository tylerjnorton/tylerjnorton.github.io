RocketReach

I joined RocketReach to build a design team from scratch. Day one it was one graphic designer stretched across everything. My job was to grow that into a real department and set up a way of working that could keep pace with engineering.

I hired a junior and a senior UX designer and moved the original designer into marketing, where his eye actually fit. To keep good people, I ran pair-design mentor sessions so newer designers learned by building next to someone stronger, and made room for the messier creative work that keeps designers around.

Then I set the process. I sat with the founders and the C-suite to map personas, journeys, and a roadmap everyone could agree on, so the team could research and wireframe ahead of the work instead of reacting to it. We leaned on Claude to prototype ideas and build design libraries online, which let us put something real in front of people in a day instead of a sprint. Every two weeks we demoed to the whole company, which kept us honest and built momentum.

A year in, the numbers backed it up: users up 35% year over year, weekly credit spend up 286% after we rebuilt the bulk-add flow from 17 clicks to 3, and ARR up 78%.
