Unqork

Unqork is a no-code platform in New York that lets big companies build enterprise software without writing code. I led design for the internal systems and services those teams ran on, with a team of three reporting to me.

Most of the job was the design system. I rebuilt our Figma system to match what the platform could really do and put real work into the documentation, because a system nobody understands is just decoration. Clean docs and honest components meant the whole team moved faster.

The rest was people and process. I mentored my designers, worked with the UX engineering and product leads on what got built and when, and ran a curriculum teaching others how to lead a good UX session. It all comes back to design thinking: stay curious, keep people at the center, and don't marry your first idea.

By the time I left, the system, the docs, and the process were pulling the same direction. Unqork hit double-unicorn status at a $2 billion valuation while growing north of 250%.
